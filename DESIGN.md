## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rare elegance and transcendental mystery of a celestial coming-of-age celebration. Tailored for an exclusive debutante experience (15 anos), the digital invitation and guest portal merge regal astronomical tradition with modern bespoke luxury.

The emotional tone balances reverence, starlight wonder, and royal sophistication. Guests feel invited not merely to a birthday party, but to an ethereal astronomical convergence. The aesthetic style is a synthesis of **Tactile Luxury & Glassmorphic Starlight**: deep abyssal night skies layered with ultra-fine, double-ruled metallic gold borders, stardust particle depth, delicate filigree, and celestial geometry honoring sun, crescent moon, and ringed orbital arcs.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the infinite depth of deep cosmic navy and midnight blue, punctuated by brilliant incandescent gold leaf accents.

- **Background & Canvas:** Dominant night canvas `#050b1e` with mid-surface elevation stepping up through `#0a1332` and `#111d4a`.
- **Primary Celestial Gold (`#f6ca65`):** The primary starburst hue, used for focal monograms, illuminated titles, and radiant call-to-actions.
- **Secondary Antique Leaf (`#d4af37`):** The grounding metallic hue, utilized for filigree borders, constellation line work, and secondary iconography.
- **Tertiary Starlight Radiance (`#ffe082`):** Specular highlight tone applied to micro-stars, active glowing rings, and glowing typography edges.
- **Support & Atmospheric Tints:** Translucent starlight overlays (`rgba(246, 202, 101, 0.12)`) and cosmic nebular dust blues (`#1a2b5c`). Contrast text renders in crisp starlight ivory (`#fcfaf2`) and muted lunar mist (`#95a5c6`).

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy strikes a delicate dialogue between timeless haute-couture ceremonial tradition and modern digital legibility.

- **Primary Display & Headings:** `Playfair Display` provides stately transitional serifs, high stroke contrast, and romantic elegance reminiscent of bespoke foil-pressed royal stationary. Display headings leverage deliberate letter-spacing and italicized poetic phrases for accents (e.g., date, guest names, debutante title).
- **Body & Functional Reading:** `Manrope` balances the classical display with clean geometric proportions, assuring that event logistics, RSVP forms, venue maps, and dress codes remain effortless to read at small sizes against dark backdrops.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Small caps and extended tracking (`0.12em` to `0.15em`) are systematically used on datelines, countdown tags, and status markers to emulate gold-leaf stamp engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on ceremonial symmetry and generous breathing room, avoiding dense utilitarian clutter in favor of classical poster proportions.

- **Grid Structure:** A centered 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (max container width: `1140px`), collapsing into 4 responsive columns on mobile devices.
- **Editorial Margins:** Outer frames maintain an architectural double-lined gold border set inset by `margin` (`3rem` desktop, `1.25rem` mobile), keeping all content suspended inside a defined celestial enclosure.
- **Rhythm & Staggering:** Section gaps scale linearly with `space-xl` for monumental pauses between the monogram hero, event narrative, RSVP, and schedule tiers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use aggressive drop shadows; instead, it relies on cosmic luminescence, translucent glass tiers, and metallic foil reflections.

1. **Abyssal Base (Ground):** Pure `#050b1e` punctuated by subtle radial gradients of deep cosmic indigo (`#0a1332`) and faint pinpoint stardust.
2. **Ethereal Glass Plates (Layer 1):** Semi-translucent panels (`rgba(10, 19, 50, 0.65)`) with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a hairline `1px` border of antique gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
3. **Floating Emblems & Cards (Layer 2):** Elevated card containers (`rgba(17, 29, 74, 0.75)`) enriched with an internal stardust glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 35px rgba(246, 202, 101, 0.12), inset 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 224, 130, 0.2)`).
4. **Radiant Focal Highlights (Layer 3):** Monograms, gold wax seals, and primary buttons emit an outward soft solar halo (`0 0 24px rgba(246, 202, 101, 0.45)`).

## Shapes

The architectural envelope relies on understated crispness softened by aristocratic refinement:

- **Border Curves:** Level `1` (Soft) is universally applied. Standard containers, inputs, and cards feature a minimal `0.25rem` (`4px`) or `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius to preserve the formal structure of luxury parchment and gilded stationery.
- **Corner Filigree & Insets:** Key presentation cards feature architectural notched corners or nested inset gold borders with ornamental four-point star icons anchored at the intersections.
- **Circular Monograms:** Hero medallions, lunar dials, and photo frames break the rectangular framework with pristine `50%` spherical geometry, surrounded by concentric thin golden rings.

## Components

### Monogram & Celestial Seal
- Centered signature emblem featuring interlaced initials ("SF") flanked by radiating sunburst rays and the crescent moon profile.
- Rendered in linear SVG stroke gold `#f6ca65` with a delicate subtext "15 anos" in Playfair Display.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Gilded pill or soft rectangle (`4px` radius) with a linear metallic gradient from `#ffe082` through `#f6ca65` to `#d4af37`. Text in midnight blue `#050b1e` with bold `Playfair Display` uppercase lettering. Hover triggers an expanded celestial gold bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 28px rgba(246, 202, 101, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Ritual Action:** Transparent midnight fill framed in a dual-line gold border (`1px solid #d4af37`), text in `#ffe082`, fading to `rgba(246, 202, 101, 0.1)` on hover.

### Celestial Cards
- Navy glass background (`rgba(10, 19, 50, 0.7)`) layered with fine double-border lines (`#d4af37`).
- Four corner star ornaments (✦) anchored `6px` inside each vertex.
- Subtle inner constellation watermark lines connecting key itinerary items.

### Inputs & RSVP Fields
- Understated nocturnal inputs with dark translucent background (`rgba(5, 11, 30, 0.6)`).
- Bottom-border only or fine continuous `1px` outline in `#324474`, transitions to glowing `#f6ca65` upon focus with zero heavy outline rings.
- Placeholder copy in muted celestial grey `#7b8fb5`, shifting to `#ffe082` floating labels.

### Checkboxes & Attendance Radios
- Replaced by custom celestial glyphs: unselected states display a fine-line crescent moon; selected states illuminate into a radiant, filled golden sunburst.

### Timeline & Countdown Dials
- Itinerary displayed along an orbital golden axis with planet-like milestones.
- Real-time countdown timer segmented in etched golden circular bezels, displaying days, hours, and minutes until the ball begins.
